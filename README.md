<h2>Prognostic lncRNA Signature in High-Grade Serous Ovarian Cancer</h2>

| # | Project | Question | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Differential expression | Which lncRNAs differ between resistant and sensitive patients? | 554 at raw p < 0.05, none survive FDR correction |
| 02 | Survival risk score | Does a composite of five lncRNAs stratify survival? | log-rank p = 1.2 × 10⁻⁹, roughly a 2-year median survival gap |
| 03 | Time-dependent ROC | Does the lncRNA signature beat FIGO stage and age predictions of disease outcome? | 5-year AUC 0.748 against 0.480 (age) and 0.471 (stage) |
| 04 | Pathway enrichment | Which biological pathways are associated with the lncRNA signature? | Overwhelmingly immune, cytokine and T-cell signaling |
